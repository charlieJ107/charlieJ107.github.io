## Who can use the platform?

The Cardiff University COMAT GCG Cluster serves a specific research group at Cardiff University. Access is arranged for authorised group members through the group's Microsoft Teams channels.

## Finding the platform and user guides

Start with the [quick start](/projects/gcg-cluster/docs/quick-start/) for workspace creation and the [Dev Containers series](/projects/gcg-cluster/docs/devcontainers-first-workspace/) for project environments. The guides also cover [storage](/projects/gcg-cluster/docs/storage-and-lifecycle/) and [GPU queueing](/projects/gcg-cluster/docs/gpu-and-queueing/).

The group's Teams channels provide the platform address, account arrangements and current starter files. If you cannot find them, ask a platform administrator in that channel.

Use the platform address and current instructions supplied there. Check the group's maintenance notices before starting work that depends on platform availability.

## Asking for help

Send the administrator enough context to locate the problem: what you were trying to do, when it happened and which environment you selected. Use the group's approved support channel for workspace details or diagnostic output, and follow the administrator's guidance on which information to share.

For larger experiments or specialised environments, discuss your compute, memory and storage needs with the administrators while planning the work.

## Understanding the design

The public design documents explain the engineering behind the platform:

- [Workspace and GPU resource design](/projects/gcg-cluster/docs/workspace-design/) covers environment reproducibility, data lifecycles and shared or exclusive GPU access.
- [Reproducible operations and observability](/projects/gcg-cluster/docs/operations-design/) covers configuration management, monitoring, maintenance and recovery considerations.

For the project's background and maintenance responsibilities, return to the [project overview](/projects/gcg-cluster/).
