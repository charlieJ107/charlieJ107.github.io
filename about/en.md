I like starting with a concrete problem and gradually turning loosely defined needs into a system people can use. From robotics and deep learning tools to virtual characters and shared computing platforms, I keep learning how to connect different layers of technology.

I care about how systems work in practice: whether people understand them, whether they are willing to trust them, and whether they can be maintained over time. Working with collaborators from different backgrounds has also taught me to pay closer attention to how experiences and concerns translate into specific design choices.

Many of my personal projects grow out of everyday needs and reflect the importance I place on privacy, control over my own data, and reliable tools. I want the things I build to remain useful over time, and to be understandable to the people who take them forward.
